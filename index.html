Our Heritage

About Vishvas Jewellers

Founded over 15 years ago at Gandhi Complex, Dindiyal Chowk, Vishvas Jewellers has grown from a family workshop into one of Gujarat's most trusted names in fine jewellery. Our name — Vishvas — means trust, and that promise is embedded in every piece we create.

We believe jewellery is more than ornamentation. It is an heirloom, a memory, and a reflection of the person who wears it. That is why our master craftsmen combine time-honoured techniques with contemporary design sensibilities to deliver pieces that transcend trends.

Our Mission

To craft jewellery that honours tradition while embracing modern elegance, ensuring every customer feels valued and confident.

Our Vision

To be recognised as India's most trusted luxury jewellery house, known for purity, craftsmanship, and exceptional service.

Craftsmanship

The Art of Creation

Our Craftsmanship Journey

From raw gold to a finished masterpiece, every Vishvas creation follows a meticulous six-step process guided by master artisans.

Step 01

Gold Selection

We source only BIS-certified gold bars and verify purity through rigorous testing before any design work begins.

Step 02

Design

Our designers sketch each piece by hand or in CAD, balancing aesthetic beauty with structural integrity and wearability.

Step 03

Handcraft

Skilled artisans shape, solder, and set each element by hand, preserving techniques passed down through generations.

Step 04

Polishing

Multiple stages of fine polishing bring out the natural lustre of gold and the fire of each diamond.

Step 05

Quality Check

Every piece undergoes a 12-point quality inspection covering weight, finish, stone security, and hallmark verification.

Step 06

Delivery

Your jewellery is elegantly packaged and presented with certification documents, ready to become a cherished heirloom.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about purchasing, caring for, and trusting your Vishvas Jewellers experience.

Yes, every gold piece at Vishvas Jewellers is crafted from BIS-hallmarked gold. We provide the hallmark certificate with each purchase, and you can verify the hallmark at any BIS-assaying centre across India.

All our diamonds above 0.30 carats come with IGI (International Gemological Institute) certification. The certificate details the cut, colour, clarity, and carat weight, ensuring complete transparency and authenticity.

We offer lifetime exchange on all Vishvas Jewellers purchases at prevailing gold rates, minus a nominal making charge. Our buyback policy ensures you receive fair market value for your gold, with transparent weight and purity verification at the time of transaction.
